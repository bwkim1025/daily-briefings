#### Context

(출처: [WSJ 9/16](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/trump-says-allowing-canada-eu), [Reuters 9/17](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-warns-eu-tariffs-canada), [Politico EU 9/16](https://www.politico.eu/article/germany-to-send-fighter-jets-latvia))
